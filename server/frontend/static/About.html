About CarDeal

CarDeal is a nationwide dealership directory that helps buyers compare showrooms, read verified customer reviews and choose with confidence.

Our story

CarDeal started with a simple observation: buying a car is stressful when you cannot tell which dealerships actually keep their promises. We bring dealer listings, customer reviews and automatic sentiment analysis into one transparent directory.

Our team combines dealership experience with software engineering. From Brooklyn to Berkeley, we work with independent showrooms to publish accurate inventory, hours and contact details, and we never remove an honest negative review.

This site is a working sample application built for the IBM Full Stack Software Developer capstone. It demonstrates a Django backend, React frontend, a Node.js and MongoDB dealership service, and a Flask sentiment-analysis service.
